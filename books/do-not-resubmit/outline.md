# Outline: Do Not Resubmit

Target total: about 14,900 words (ch1 is 1,057, ch2–7 about 2,300 each). Single POV: Mira, close third person, past tense.
The percentage after each chapter is where the chapter ends in the book.

## Structure profile (story-structure Map, 2026-10-16)

- Hook: ch1, first line (a dentist confused about being a teapot)
- Inciting Event (~12%): ch2, first third. The notice is renewed, and Agnes Hale is on tomorrow's roster.
- Key Event (end of Act 1): ch2, end. Mira trades shifts with Dot; her name is logged against Agnes's intake.
- First Plot Point (~24%): ch3, opening. Mira takes the intake of someone who knew her mother. Doorway #1 check: she could hand Agnes back to Dot, and doesn't.
- First Pinch Point (~37%): ch3, end. Pell rejects the resubmission: "one testimony carries this case", "I sealed it for a reason."
- Midpoint / Moment of Truth (~52%): ch4, end. The heaviest testimony is Mira's own. Mirror Moment check: "Who am I? I'm the one who put her there." This is a change in how she sees herself, not just a twist.
- Second Pinch Point (~62%): ch5, middle. Pell names the cost: a personal appeal opens her own file. She could come out a doormat.
- Third Plot Point (~69%): ch5, end. The cold chair, then she finds the left-out memories and files the appeal under her own name. Doorway #2 check: once filed, her file is open, so there's no way back.
- Third Act Turning Point (~85%): ch6, end. Ruth's file is weighed with Mira's additions but the verdict is held, because Mira's own file must be weighed in the same sitting. Pell breaks the seal on her drawer.
- Climactic Moment (~90–95%): ch7, first half. Ruth's testimony about Mira is read aloud. Passage is granted to both. Final Battle check: settles the psychological "death stakes" set in ch1 (indefinite; Mira's own deferral). Transformation check: Mira asks for it in her own words, and sits *in* the warmth instead of beside it (ch7, end).
- Resolution: ch7, second half. The flat, the chair, tea, walking out.
- Optional signposts planned: Care Package (ch1, the evening with the chair), Argument Against Transformation (ch1, "I don't know how they do that. Just... accept it."), Trouble Brewing (ch2, Dot's passage gossip, and the clock humming at Ruth's file number), Pet the Dog (ch4, Mira keeps the clock off the porters' list and promises it a place near the Archive), Lights Out (ch5, the cold cushion), Q Factor (ch1, the warm lap pays off in ch6 testimony and ch7 reunion).
- Last structure check: Map, 2026-10-16

## Ch1: Intake *(written, 1,057 words, ~7%)*
- **Goal / conflict / turn:** routine intake, the dentist accepts teapot. At home, the warm chair; the notice; "new evidence".
- **Beats:** Hook, Care Package, Argument Against Transformation.
- **Closing hook:** she doesn't let herself think, *not yet*, about where she might find it.

## Ch2: The Queue *(~2,300 words, ~23%)*
- **Goal:** find someone newly dead who knew Ruth.
- **Conflict:** clerks may not read the incoming roster, and the porters come for the shelf objects. Mira slips the mantel clock off the collection list and takes it home ("a clerical delay"). It hums when she says Ruth's file number aloud for the first time.
- **Scenes:**
  1. Morning: the seasonal notice arrives early, the same words again. It makes her angry, which is new.
  2. Intake, between souls: Dot gossips about a man last decade who "walked out in his own shape". This is **passage**, and Mira dismisses it.
  3. Lunch: Mira reads the roster on Dot's desk. Agnes Hale, Flat 1, of the street Mira grew up on, is due tomorrow.
  4. She trades her morning shift for Dot's. Dot asks no questions and wants two weeks of tea runs in return.
- **Turn:** the trade is logged under her name. If anyone checks, it's obvious.
- **Beats:** Inciting Event (scenes 1–3), Trouble Brewing, Key Event.
- **Closing hook:** at home she puts the clock on the sill beside the chair. The chair creaks; the clock hums back.

## Ch3: Witness *(~2,300 words, ~38%)*
- **Goal:** get Agnes's memories of Ruth on record before Agnes's shape fades.
- **Conflict:** Agnes is old and drifting and remembers Ruth only in fragments: always tired, always on the stairs, gone at night. That's no help, until she adds that Ruth asked her to leave the stair light on for "the little one" on work nights, every night for years. Mira didn't know.
- **Scenes:**
  1. Agnes in the chair: "You're Ruth's girl." Mira has her open shape and her open file and a pen that won't hold still.
  2. The testimony. Mira learns how to take a statement properly, adding a memory to someone else's file. Rule shown: **add, never delete**.
  3. "You want to appeal?" Agnes is a hall lamp and is pleased. "I always liked being the one who left it on." Pet-the-dog warmth; she fades.
  4. Mira files the resubmission. Within the hour (time like weather) she's called up to Pell's office.
  5. Pell, kind and immovable: one kind memory can't outweigh "the principal testimony". He won't say whose it is.
- **Turn:** the rejection, and the existence of a single principal testimony.
- **Beats:** First Plot Point (scene 1), First Pinch Point (scene 5).
- **Closing hook:** "Leave it, Mira. I sealed it for a reason."

## Ch4: The Archive *(~2,300 words, ~53%)*
- **Goal:** read the sealed file.
- **Conflict:** the Archive is off-limits to intake clerks, and it shifts like weather. The clock guides her, humming warmer and ticking colder, through the quiet hour.
- **Scenes:**
  1. Night. Mira carries the clock to the Office in her bag. Its ticking echoes in the empty corridors.
  2. The Archive: dust, cold stone, brass tags. The hot/cold walk. Near a run of drawers marked with living names, the clock hums oddly. (Rule shown: **drawers for people still alive**.)
  3. She finds Ruth's file and reads Agnes's new page, then Ruth's own appeal statement: "I was not there enough. I would like that weighed against the rest, if it can be."
  4. The principal testimony: a list of absences. The missed recital, the cold dinners, the locked door in the morning. Every one is true, and every one is recorded in her own voice.
  5. On the way out, the clock stops at a drawer: *MIRA*, sealed with a clerk's seal, thicker than she'd have guessed.
- **Turn / Mirror Moment:** she put her mother in the chair.
- **Beats:** Pet the Dog (scene 1, she promises the clock a place within earshot of the Archive), Midpoint.
- **Closing hook:** home, she reaches for the cushion, and for the first time it is cold.

## Ch5: The Cold Chair *(~2,300 words, ~69%)*
- **Goal:** work out whether her testimony was a lie, and what she can do now.
- **Conflict:** it wasn't a lie. Testimonies can't be withdrawn. Pell finds her (he knows about the Archive and says nothing about it) and tells her the price.
- **Scenes:**
  1. A day of intakes done badly. She can't ask "Want to appeal?" without her voice going. Dot notices.
  2. Pell's office, the other side of the desk. He explains the kin rule, the seal, the chair he had placed in her flat "so you'd have somewhere to sit". A personal appeal opens her own file. He has seen clerks come out as doormats. "Your drawer is sealed even from me. I don't know what's in it, and neither do you." (Second Pinch Point.)
  3. Night. She can't sit by the chair. She sits across the room, which is worse. (Lights Out.)
  4. She makes herself remember on purpose, not the list but the whole of it: the lap, warm at seven in the morning after a night shift. A nurse's cardigan smelling of hospital soap. Notes on the kitchen table. A light left on in the stairwell.
  5. Before dawn she writes the appeal under her own name.
- **Turn:** she files it. Her file is now open.
- **Beats:** Second Pinch Point, Lights Out, Third Plot Point.
- **Closing hook:** the stamp comes back: *Personal appeal received. Clerk's deferral suspended. Hearing set.*

## Ch6: The Hearing *(~2,300 words, ~85%)*
- **Goal:** change the record without lying.
- **Conflict:** Pell is the Office's advocate and must read her principal testimony aloud, word for word, in a cold room in front of a weighing panel. (Mounting Forces: the panel, the procedure, the weight of four years.)
- **Scenes:**
  1. The hearing room. Dot is in the gallery, having swapped her own shift. The chair has been carried in by porters and stands in the middle of the room.
  2. Pell reads. Each absence lands. Mira doesn't contest a single one.
  3. Mira's addition, one memory at a time: the stair light (citing Agnes), the notes, the lap, the cardigan. "All of that is true too. I just didn't count it."
  4. The panel weighs Ruth's file. The scales are written as sound: the chair creaks, long and low.
  5. The verdict is held. A personal appeal ties two files together, so Mira's own file must be weighed in the same sitting.
- **Turn:** Pell breaks the seal on her drawer.
- **Beats:** Third Act Turning Point.
- **Closing hook:** Pell turns the first page, and stops. "This one isn't yours. It's hers."

## Ch7: Passage *(~2,300 words, 100%)*
- **Goal:** get through her own weighing, whatever she becomes.
- **Climax:**
  1. Pell reads Ruth's testimony about Mira, carried into the Office four years ago and waiting in the drawer ever since. Ruth stood in Mira's doorway every evening before work, every single one. She asked Agnes about the stair light. She kept every drawing. Mira's absences are in it too: a daughter who stopped phoning. It's weighed, it's whole, and it's kind.
  2. The panel confers. Ruth's term is ended. Two stamps, the same word on both: *Passage granted.* Pell stamps the second one himself.
  3. The chair is warm again, and then it isn't a chair. Ruth's shape comes back: tired eyes, a nurse's cardigan, hands that know where to rest. (Transformation: Mira sits in her mother's lap the way she couldn't for three years, or simply in the room with her, not beside the object.)
- **Resolution:**
  4. Short goodbyes. Dot cries. Pell gives Mira back her own case number, stamped closed. The clock is placed on a shelf just outside the Archive, and it hums.
  5. The flat. Mira makes tea in her teapot. Ruth drinks it, says it's too strong, and drinks it anyway. They talk, finally, about nothing in particular.
  6. They leave the door unlocked and the light on, and walk out together through the sideways city towards whatever's next. Last line on warmth.
- **Beats:** Climactic Moment, Final Battle, Transformation, Resolution.
