# 08 · "want it" runs together in the American voice

Source: *Second-Hand Dragons* (af_heart render, 2026-10-19), the dragon's catchphrase in ch03, ch05, ch07 and ch10.
**Found by the automatic listener**: both Whisper models heard all four as "I wanted on the record".

> I noticed that. I want it on the record that I noticed it, on the second Thursday, long before anything else happened.

**Heard:** "I wanted on the record". af_heart links *want it* into one word (American flapped t), so the sentence
sounds like a past tense and the listener loses the meaning. The same voice says "I want that on the record" (ch09)
cleanly, because *that* starts with a consonant.

**Fix (applied to the manuscript):** "Let the record show that I noticed it…". Same voice for the dragon, no *want it*.

`before` = the passage as first rendered; `after` = with the fix. `heard` = the clip from ch03 at 3:54.

Status: fixed in the book; rule proposal P3 (watch for "want it", "get it", "put it" before a vowel in af_heart).
