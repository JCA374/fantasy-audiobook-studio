# 04 · A one-word hedge boxed in commas sounds choppy

Source: *The One in the Chair*, ch01, ¶8. Reported by the author.

> …the thing that passed for kindness in this job — not softness, exactly, but attention.

**Heard:** "not softness — exactly — but attention": three short stops, so the contrast *not X but Y* falls apart.

**Author's fix:** "not softness exactly, but attention". One pause, before *but*.

**Rule (speech-flow):** drop the commas around a one-word hedge (exactly, really, quite, perhaps…) when it sits right before *but / and / or / yet*. This is the opposite of case 02's "by all accounts", where a **multi-word** interrupter mid-clause needs commas to breathe. New scanner check: `hedge boxed in commas (choppy)`.
